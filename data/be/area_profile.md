## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Belgium does not currently have a general annual net wealth tax, though it is one of the most hotly contested issues in Belgian politics.
- Securities Account Tax (*Taxe sur les comptes-titres* / *Effectentaks*):
  - In 2018, the Michel I government enacted a 0.15% tax on securities accounts exceeding €500,000, struck down by the Constitutional Court in October 2019 for unequal treatment.
  - In 2021, the De Croo (Vivaldi) government introduced an annual solidarity tax of 0.15% on securities accounts holding €1 million or more (Law of 17 February 2021). Classified under `partial_wealth`.
- Net Wealth Tax proposals:
  - **PTB-PVDA (Taxe des millionnaires / Miljonairstaks):** Flagship campaign since 2009. The proposal was detailed in books by Peter Mertens (*Op mensenmaat*, 2009; *De Miljonairstaks*, 2015) and repeatedly tabled as legislative proposals in the federal Chamber by Marco Van Hees, Raoul Hedebouw, and Sofie Merckx (notably in 2014, 2019, 2020 during COVID-19, and updated in 2024). The current model proposes a 2% tax on net wealth between €5 million and €10 million, and 3% on net wealth over €10 million, exempting 99% of the population.
  - **PS (Parti Socialiste):** Co-initiator (led by party president Paul Magnette) of the European Citizens' Initiative (ECI) "Tax the rich: Créer un impôt européen sur la grande fortune pour financer la transition écologique et sociale" (registered with the EU Commission in 2023). PS officially advocates an EU-wide and Belgian progressive wealth tax targeting the top fortunes / billionaires, drawing on Gabriel Zucman's 2% proposal.
  - **Groen:** Proposes a progressive wealth tax (*miljonairsbelasting*) on net wealth above €2.5 million: 0.4% between €2.5m and €5m, 0.8% between €5m and €10m, and 1.2% over €10m.
  - **Vooruit:** Launched an official petition (*Tijd voor een miljonairstaks*) proposing a 0.3% levy on net fortunes over €1 million (family home exempt). Strongly championed by Conner Rousseau.
  - **Ecolo:** Calls for an *impôt sur les grandes fortunes* and supports the EU wealth tax initiative.
- Opponents: MR (Mouvement Réformateur, Georges-Louis Bouchez), Open Vld, N-VA, and Vlaams Belang strongly oppose any wealth tax (*vermogensbelasting*), arguing it causes capital flight and amounts to double taxation.
- Polls: Dedicated / Ipsos surveys for *Le Vif* and CNCD-11.11.11 regularly find around 80% of Belgians support a wealth tax on net fortunes exceeding €1 million (excluding the primary residence).

## 5. Government below national level
- Belgium is divided into 3 Regions (Flemish Region, Walloon Region, Brussels-Capital Region) and 10 Provinces (5 Flemish, 5 Walloon; Brussels has no provincial tier).
- Local tier: 581 municipalities (*communes* / *gemeenten*). Municipalities have fiscal powers regarding additional surcharges on personal income tax (*opcentiemen*) and property withholding tax (*onroerende voorheffing*), but national wealth taxes fall under federal jurisdiction.
- Register & Map: Official REFNIS / NIS codes from Statbel / Eurostat GISCO LAU 2024. All 581 municipalities are mapped and matched.
- Local decision: Seed pass focuses on federal and regional bodies; local sweep will sample major city councils (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, Charleroi, Liège).
