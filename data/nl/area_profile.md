# Area profile — Netherlands

Seed pass, 22 October 2026 (R001). Depth: Seed, national level. Languages: Dutch, English.

## 1. Scope
National (Tweede Kamer, government, parties, NGOs, unions, economists, public wealthy). Not covered: Eerste Kamer, Dutch MEPs, the 12 provinces and 342 municipalities (all loaded in the register, none swept).

## 2. History and live proposals
- The Netherlands has no net wealth tax. Box 3 of income tax taxes a deemed return on savings and investments; the Hoge Raad rulings (2021-2024) forced a move to actual returns, now in the Wet werkelijk rendement box 3 (36 748). Support for box 3 reform alone is not counted here.
- "Miljonairsbelasting": SP amendment 2014 (0.7% extra above EUR 1m), SP/PvdA/GL motions 2015-2019 (above EUR 1m), SP 2025 programme (5% above EUR 5m, wealth cap above EUR 50m). [Tweede Kamer open data; Accountancy Vanmorgen]
- Global billionaire tax: motions Alkaya and Van der Lee adopted 25 October 2023 with broad support, including VVD, CDA, PVV and ChristenUnie. Those parties are not recorded as supporters because the motions ask for international action only.
- Gabriel Zucman spoke to the Tweede Kamer finance committee in early February 2026 at GL-PvdA's invitation (2% above EUR 100m). [NOS]
- Government replies (Feb-March 2026) say the Oxfam and Zucman proposals are hard to carry out and that wealth inequality fell 2011-2024.
