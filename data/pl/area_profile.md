# Poland

## 1. Scope
Initial national seed, 22 October 2026: four supporters and six evidence rows.
This is an incomplete first pass. All evidence remains verified=n pending
maintainer review. The author's quote check found all six retained quotes.
Polish and English discovery searches were used; local advocacy is not searched.

## 2. Confirmed party proposals
[Razem's 20 January 2020 statement](https://partiarazem.pl/stanowiska/2020/01/20/jak-multimilionerzy-moga-pomoc-w-ratowaniu-klimatu)
proposes a progressive annual wealth tax above PLN 10m, rates of 1-8%, a wealth
register and a linked exit levy. The unnamed 17 councillors at its conclusion
are not a parliamentary roll call and were not imported as individual actors.
The [economic manifesto](https://partiarazem.pl/deklaracja-programowa/3-gospodarka-dla-ludzi)
backs an EU tax on large fortunes. Its [parent declaration](https://partiarazem.pl/deklaracja-programowa)
dates adoption to the 8-9 November 2025 congress; the closing date is used.
Party membership alone does not establish personal endorsement.

## 3. Campaigns
[WeMove Europe's Polish campaign](https://action.wemove.eu/sign/2023-12-tax-the-rich-petition-PL)
calls for a European tax on the largest fortunes. No publication date could be corroborated; the URL identifier alone is
insufficient. The organisation is queued in harvest.csv, not included among
supporters. No petition signers are recorded. Other domestic NGOs and unions remain to be substantively checked.

## 6. Geography
Current official TERYT/TERC full register from
[Statistics Poland](https://eteryt.stat.gov.pl/eTeryt/rejestr_teryt/udostepnianie_danych/baza_teryt/uzytkownicy_indywidualni/pobieranie/pliki_pelne.aspx),
downloaded 22 October 2026, state date 1 January 2026: 16 voivodeships,
380 powiats/cities with powiat rights and 2479 gminas. Original two-, four- and
seven-digit TERYT codes are preserved. Only gmina kinds 1/2/3 are included;
components of mixed gminas and city subdivisions are excluded. Urban/rural
suffixes distinguish same-name siblings. scripts/import_pl_geography.py
reproduces this import from the official ZIP.
The initial BDL listing included historical units and was replaced in full.
Every subnational unit is pending. No local sample is complete. No map ships:
licence, date and code match of current gmina shapes remain unchecked.

## 7. Search terms and definition
Polish: podatek majatkowy (with Polish diacritics), podatek od majatku netto,
podatek od wielkich majatkow, opodatkowanie miliarderow. English: wealth tax Poland.
The Polish term also covers property/inheritance taxes. Inspect the asset base;
property-only, inheritance-only and income-only reforms do not qualify.

## 8. Source access and QA
Used agent-reach/Exa until its free service returned a rate limit, then web
search and opened source pages. Some direct reads returned HTTP 403; the FMS
host failed DNS resolution. No snippet-only actor was imported.
The initial checkquotes run found all seven submitted quotes. An independent
reviewer reopened the original sources, confirmed quotes and attribution, and
requested formal-act grade corrections and corroboration of the campaign date.
Grades were corrected and the undated campaign moved to harvest.csv. All six
retained evidence rows remain verified=n for maintainer review.

## 9. Coverage gaps

economists, wealthy/business/faith/cultural figures and regional/local sampling

claim is made. This run does not establish the full history of Polish tax law.
