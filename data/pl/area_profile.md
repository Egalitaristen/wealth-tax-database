# Poland

## Scope and depth
National research in progress, 22 October 2026: **29 supporters and 31 evidence rows**, following R001 (4/6) and R002 (25/25). This is an incomplete national pass. A few hundred supporters is the research ambition; that yield remains to be established. All evidence remains verified=n for maintainer review. Polish and English sources were searched. Membership alone is never treated as personal endorsement.

## Parties, unions and campaigns
Razem's dated 2020 statement proposes annual progressive wealth taxation above PLN 10m, with 1–8% rates. Its 2025 economic manifesto also backs an EU tax on large fortunes. The Greens' 2018 programme specifies a 5% tax on private fortunes above PLN 5m; frequency is unspecified. Małgorzata Tracz expressly reaffirmed that programme in a January 2020 interview. Young Left's programme supports a progressive wealth tax; the 2022 year is corroborated by page metadata and the programme PDF, without claiming an exact adoption day.

OPZZ and its named leader Piotr Ostrowski signed the 5 June 2024 European union letter calling for a tax on large fortunes, republished by STTK on 6 June. WeMove Europe's dated 30 April 2024 campaign update resolves the initially undated Polish petition lead. That date is the update date, not the petition launch. No private petition signers were imported.

The TDEM initial public academic roster, dated 9 December 2018, contains Karolina Borońska, identified as Karolina Borońska-Hryniewiecka through her university record. The Polish manifesto calls for wealth taxation above EUR 1m. Only the initial public roster was checked; the much larger ordinary petition list was not imported.

## Parliamentary acts
The original [12 December 2023 roll call](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:C_202500480), vote 62 on A9-0336/2023 Amendment 2, records affirmative votes by Danuta Hübner, Marek Balt, Robert Biedroń, Włodzimierz Cimoszewicz, Łukasz Kohut, Bogusław Liberadzki and Leszek Miller. The affirmative roster is on printed pages 133–134. Marek Belka is separately included as a named sponsor on 6 December, not as an affirmative voter. The amendment failed overall (232/331/49).

The [archived original amendment](https://web.archive.org/web/20231215085925id_/https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/A-9-2023-0336-AM-002-003_EN.pdf) explicitly calls for solidarity levies on high wealth and international discussions for a progressive wealth tax. Whole-report votes are not used as a proxy. Party and role changes since the act are qualified in actor notes.

## Exclusions and held leads
- The examined Piotr Wójcik column prioritises other reforms; the examined Zandberg interview concerns income tax; Kotecki's reported proposal concerns cadastral tax. These are source-specific exclusions, not claims about all their views. Fresh personal endorsements can qualify independently.
- The 2026 OKI optional investment-account wrapper is not used to import a bulk list of legislators. Its asset charge substitutes for capital-gains taxation within a savings incentive; a whole-bill vote does not establish general support for taxing wealthy people's wealth.
- Maciej Danielewicz's contextual discussion of French wealth taxation remains a held lead, not an imported endorsement.
- The FMS programme PDF could not be fetched from its original host. Search-result wording alone was not imported.
- The May 2023 EP own-resources wealth-tax amendment is queued in H0004. Search discovery found explicit household wealth-tax wording and a Biedroń sponsor lead, but the original endpoint was bot-blocked. Obtain the original amendment and its exact roll call before importing; whole-resolution votes do not suffice.

## Geography and local sampling
Official [TERYT/TERC full register](https://eteryt.stat.gov.pl/eTeryt/rejestr_teryt/udostepnianie_danych/baza_teryt/uzytkownicy_indywidualni/pobieranie/pliki_pelne.aspx), state 1 January 2026, downloaded 22 October: 16 voivodeships, 380 county-level units and 2479 gminas. Original two-, four- and seven-digit codes are retained. Only gmina kinds 1/2/3 are included, excluding components and city subdivisions. Urban/rural suffixes distinguish same-name units. scripts/import_pl_geography.py reproduces the import from the official ZIP. An initial historical BDL listing was replaced in full. Every subnational unit remains pending.

Exploratory searches around Warsaw, Kraków, Łódź, Wrocław and Poznań yielded property-tax leads, including the dead-apartments campaign, rather than clearly qualifying wealth-tax acts. This is not completed local coverage and does not justify a municipality-by-municipality expansion yet. No map ships: current gmina shapes still need licence, date and official-code matching checks.

## Evidence checks and access
R001: all six retained quotes found by checkquotes and originals independently reviewed. R002: all 16 web quotations found by checkquotes. The same run reports eight parliamentary PDF quotes not found and one programme PDF skipped: direct EP endpoints are bot-blocked or empty, and pdftotext is unavailable. These are access/extraction failures, not positive automated matches. Seven vote quotations were checked against the opened official roll-call PDF; Belka's sponsor quote against the archived original using pypdf; the Greens programme quote against its opened PDF using pypdf. Adjacent names in roster quotes give verbatim context and are not extra attributed actors.

Independent review covered the non-parliamentary additions, including over five further original sources, and requested scope, attribution and encoding corrections. Parliamentary originals were checked separately by the author. No evidence is marked independently verified. Schema validation, deduplication and the site build are checked before publishing this batch.

Agent Reach/Exa and opened web sources were used. Exa intermittently rate-limited; some pages returned 403, DNS errors, robot checks or empty PDFs. Public archives and alternate original-document hosts were used where available. No actor was imported from a search snippet alone.
